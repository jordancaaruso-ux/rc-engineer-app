# TLR 22X blank setup sheet - answer key for the drawing boxes

Sheet: `sheet_de4956b809516753` (blank.pdf, A4, signed "Arno"). It is TLR's own 22X sheet layout
with a re-made form, so many PDF field names do NOT describe their box (see "name traps" below).
Built without opening any of the naming AI's files.

## Sources
- **[M]** TLR 22X DC kit manual `TLR-1233_MANUAL_ML.pdf` (Horizon Hobby product page TLR-1233; the AC kit TLR-1234 uses the same book). Page = printed page number.
- **[K]** `TLR-1233_KIT_SETUP.pdf` = manual p54: TLR's filled **DC kit setup** on this exact layout (the strongest evidence for kit values).
- **[F]** TLR's own `TLR 22X Race Kit Setup Sheet Editable.pdf`: same drawing. Its field names at the same spots are quoted where they help (some are stale names too).
- **[T]** TLR's filled team sheets, read out of the PDF form fields: H. King + R. Moore (Dirt Nats '25), Alberico / Guest / Malott / Moore (sealed clay), Alberico + King (carpet), plus the AC kit sheet TLR-1234_KIT_SETUP.
- **[H]** Horizon product TLR-1261 "VHA Hub Body Set, Aluminum, HRC, Black: 22X".
- **[P]** Positions: each widget rectangle in blank.pdf matched to the printed label and leader line on page.png and the tagged part pictures. Every "tag -> choice" mapping below comes from this.

**Name traps (the PDF field name is wrong for the box):** Caster Angle = front kick-up; F Block Ht = caster; F Block Mat = spindle KPI; Front Drive Shim = front pivot material; Front VLA = spindle height; Steering Hub Height = front axle spacer; Front Hub Angle = camber mount A/B/C; St Rack Ball Insert = inner FRONT CAMBER-LINK insert; Steering Rack Ball = caster block N/W; Steering Hub Ball = spindle N/W; Rr Inner Camber Ht = diff height; Rr Outdrive = rear arm 74/75; Rr Drive Hex = wheel hex (this one is right); Rr Arm Material = chassis +3/STD/-3; Transmission Brace = motor plate; Transmission = rear arms; Check Box169/170 = waterfall brace; Mud Guards = side guards; Check Box168 = C pivot block.

**Tag order traps:** part 03 tag 2, part 08 tag 2 and part 11 run opposite to the printed numbers; part 05 tags 1.1/1.2 = holes 3/1; part 08 tags 10.4 = "+4" and 10.5 = "+3" (swapped).

## 2. How the 22X corners are laid out

1. Front lower arm: inner hinge pin in the front pivot ("Pivot, Bridge" TLR-1079; alu/brass options) under the bulkhead. The kick-shim plate (TLR-1075) between them is turned for 20 / 22.5 / 25 deg kick-up (kit 25). [M p16 D7, p17 D8]
2. Arm outer end: the hinge pin passes through a caster insert (TLR-1096: 0, +-2.5, +-5 deg, marked L/R) in the caster block (Wide TLR-1115 kit / Narrow TLR-1098). [M p18 E2]
3. The spindle is C-shaped and wraps the caster block on two kingpin screws in flanged bushings (TLR234117); how the bushings are stacked gives Low / Mid / High spindle height (kit Mid). Spindles come Wide (kit) / Narrow, and in alu with 2/4/6/8 deg KPI. [M p17 E1, p18 E2, parts p52]
4. Front camber link: inner ball stud (5.8x12, TLR-1151) screws into a camber insert (TLR234105: two-hole #1/#3 or single-hole #2 = kit) on the bulkhead's camber post, on a 1 mm washer. Outer ball stud (5.8x8) sits on a camber mount (A/B/C = TLR-1148/-1178/-1132, kit B) bolted to the caster block, on 1 + 2 mm washers. [M p15 D4, p18 E2]
5. Steering: two bellcranks + connecting link (TLR-1077) on the chassis; each tie-rod's inner ball stud (5.8x6) hangs under a bellcrank arm (no washer in kit). Outer ball stud (5.8x8) on a bolt-on steering arm (TLR-1118 "A", Wide; Narrow TLR-1089) under the spindle, on 0.5 + 1 mm washers. Bump steer = washers under either ball, or steering-arm spacers TLR-1136. [M p13 D1, p17 E1]
6. Front shocks: top in 1 of 5 holes across the carbon tower (-2 / STD / +2; kit middle), bottom in 1 of 3 holes on the arm (kit middle). Front axle TLR-1156 (hex flange) runs in two bearings in the spindle. [M p15-17]
7. Rear gearbox: diff height from bearing inserts (TLR-1106 set, 5 positions, kit 4th). The rear camber block (TLR-1138) bolts on top of the gearbox and carries both inner camber ball studs (5.8x6) on 2 mm washers; the sheet numbers its holes 1-3 (kit 2). [M p25 F8-F9]
8. Rear arms (V2, moulded "75") pivot on 3.5x51 mm pins through pill inserts (TLR234132) in the C block (front; alu kit, brass option) and D block (rear, TLR-1150). Centre dot = 2 deg anti-squat, 3 deg toe; #.5 pill moves the pin 0.375 mm (0.5 deg), #1 pill 0.75 mm (1 deg); up/down = roll centre, in/out = pivot width. Kit spacers on the inner pin: 2 x 1 mm in front of the arm, 0.5 mm behind. [M p28 G4A, p29 G4B]
9. Rear hub = TLR 22 5.0 VHA hub (composite kit; alu; HRC alu TLR-1261). Its bottom slot takes a VHA hinge-pin insert (0 to +4 mm, kit +2) = axle height against the arm. 0.5+0.5 mm and 1+1 mm washers on the outer pin place the hub fore/aft (kit 1 mm front, 2 mm rear). [M p27 G2, p28 G3; H]
10. Outer rear camber ball stud (5.8x6, 1 mm washer) sits in hole A, B or C of the VHA camber-link mount (TLR334074) on top of the hub (kit B). [M p27 G2]
11. Rear axle (VHA rear axle) has a CVA cup with 4 cross-pin holes (kit 1) for the CVA bone (X66-X69, kit X67) or a universal shaft. Wheel hex TLR232112 stamped 4.5 mm (kit). [M p27 G1]
12. Rear shocks: top in 1 of 4 holes on the carbon tower (-2 / STD / +2), bottom on an arm standoff (TLR-1179), outside or inside (kit inside). [K; M p31]
13. Chassis: 2 mm alu plate STD / +3 / -3 (TLR-1173/-1163/-1164). Side guards Soft (kit) / Standard / Stiffezel. Battery holders (TLR-1097) screw into 1 of 5 holes along the side (kit 3). Electronics tray, motor plate and waterfall brace each come in 2-3 materials. [M p14, p23, p30, p35-36; parts p45-53]

## 3. Questions a racer could settle

1. Part 10 tag 1 (Text70): what is the spacer on top of the rear hub: spacers under the VHA camber-link mount? (TLR always writes 0.)
2. Part 19 tag 1 (Text143, "___ mm" at the servo): servo-horn length, or a servo-mount offset?
3. Part 03 tag 10 (A/B/C): is this the camber mount A/B/C on the caster block (what the manual shows), or the steering-link hole (TLR's own form calls this field "ST Link Attach")?
4. Part 03 tag 4 (1.0 / 0.5 / 0.0): is it a spacer on the front axle between spindle and wheel hex (front track width)?
5. Part 03 tag 7 (0.7 / 1.7 / 2.7 mm): is this the manual's Low / Mid / High spindle-bushing height (kit Mid = 1.7)?
6. Part 08 tag 9 (74 / 75): is this the number moulded on the rear arm (75 = V2 standard, 74 = narrow arm)?
7. Part 08 tag 10 (0 to +4 under the rear view): is it the diff-height insert position (kit +3)?
8. Part 05 tag 1: do camber-insert holes 1 and 3 move the inner ball stud in/out or fore/aft, and which way is 1?
9. Part 12: is the right-hand column of the C/D grids the inboard side? Are the 8 cells with no TLR pill ever used?
10. Part 06 tag 4 (Text49): spacer between spindle and steering arm (TLR-1136), or washers under the flanged nut?
11. What KPI is the kit composite spindle? (TLR leaves 2/4/6/8 blank on its kit sheet.)
12. Upper shock holes: is hole 5 (front) / hole 4 (rear) the outermost?
