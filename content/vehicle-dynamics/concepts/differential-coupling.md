## Differential coupling
**Physics.** A differential lets the two **driven** wheels turn at **different speeds** — which they
must through a corner, since the outer wheel travels further. **Coupling** is how strongly the diff
**resists** that speed difference: **free** (open) at one extreme, fully **locked** (a spool — both
wheels forced to one speed) at the other. On power, more coupling drives the two wheels **more
together**, forcing the loaded outer and lighter inner toward the same speed against the different
speeds the arc asks for.

**The window (founder, 2026-08-27).** On-power and off-power move opposite ways with coupling, so each
end of the window fails on both at once. **Too much coupling (too thick):** off-power understeer and
on-power oversteer. **Too little (too thin):** off-power oversteer and on-power understeer — and it
lacks the drive a thicker diff would have. A driver describing one of the four should be asked about
its partner before the diff is blamed: a thick diff shows as both, never one.
