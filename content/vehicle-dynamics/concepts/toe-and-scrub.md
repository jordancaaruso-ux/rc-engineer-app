## Toe and scrub
**Physics.** A toed wheel runs a small **slip angle** going straight, so it makes side force before
you steer — with **toe-in** the two wheels' forces point **toward** each other, with **toe-out**
**apart**, so they cancel in a straight line. The same slip **scrubs**: drag and heat, more with
more toe. Toe also shifts through **travel** (bump steer / toe gain) and, at the front, as you
**steer** — what the tyre runs is the sum, not the sheet value.

**Toe-out subtracts from the outer wheel's steer angle; toe-in adds to it** — and the outer wheel is
the one that counts ([[loaded-wheel]]).
