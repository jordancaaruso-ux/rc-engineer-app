## Load transfer

**Physics.** Under cornering, braking or power, vertical load shifts across the tyres — **laterally**
(inside → outside in a corner) and **fore-aft** (rear → front under brakes, front → rear on power). The
**total** is set by **CoG height** with **track width** (lateral) or **wheelbase** (fore-aft) and the g;
most knobs only change *how fast* and *where* it moves — a few move the total: track width, and anything
that changes **CoG** (ride height, ballast, chassis).

Two facets the setup shapes:
- **Speed** — load reaches the loaded tyres two ways: through the **links / geometry** it transfers
  **fast** (near-instant, "geometric"); through the **springs**, **slow** ("elastic"). Higher **roll
  centre** sends more the fast way laterally; **anti-dive / anti-squat** does the same fore-aft. This
  speed is what sets [[bite-hold]].
- **Front/rear split** — the lateral total can transfer more at the front or the rear, set by the
  front/rear roll-stiffness balance.

**Affects:** [[bite-hold]] (via speed).
**Moved by:** roll centre [[roll-center]] & anti-dive/anti-squat [[anti-dive-anti-squat]] (fast fraction),
springs / ARBs [[roll-stiffness]] (split & elastic path), track width & CoG / ride height / weight (the total).
