## Aerodynamic load
**Physics.** The bodyshell (and any wing) makes an **aerodynamic vertical load** as air flows over it,
and that force grows with the **square of speed**: double the speed, roughly **four times** the load. So
it adds real grip in **fast** sections and **almost nothing** slow. Where it acts — more front or rear —
biases grip at speed; and because it scales with speed², that **balance itself shifts with speed**.

**Moved by:** bodyshell height / position, body choice.
