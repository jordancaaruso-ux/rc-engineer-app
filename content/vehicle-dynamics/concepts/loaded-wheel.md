## The loaded wheel
**Physics.** An axle's side force is the **sum of its two wheels'**, and each wheel's force scales
with the **vertical load** it carries ([[tyre-load-sensitivity]]). Whenever the axle is making side
force the **outer** wheel carries the larger share ([[load-transfer]]), so what changes the outer
wheel's **angle, camber or grip** moves the axle; the same change at the inner wheel moves it much
less.
