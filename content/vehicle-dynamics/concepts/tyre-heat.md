## Tyre heat — what makes a tyre work
**Concepts:** [[bite-hold]], [[corner-regime]], [[toe-and-scrub]]

**Physics.** A tyre heats from the work it does — the force it carries times how far it slides — so it
heats more the more of each corner it spends loaded, and the harder it is loaded. Stiffer in roll — a
stiffer bar or spring, a higher roll centre, less flex — loads the tyres sooner, so they work for more
of every corner ([[bite-hold]]; founder, 2026-09-23); made at one end only, that end also takes a
larger share of the load transfer, so its outside tyre is loaded harder than before ([[corner-regime]]).
Toe heats a tyre by scrubbing it ([[toe-and-scrub]]).
