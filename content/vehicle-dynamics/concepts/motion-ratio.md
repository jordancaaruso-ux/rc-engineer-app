## Motion ratio
**Physics.** How far the **spring/damper** moves per unit of **wheel** travel — shock travel over
wheel travel, the same definition [[shock-geometry]] uses, so a lower ratio is a unit moving less
than the wheel. It is set by where the unit mounts on the arm. A unit mounted further inboard moves
less than the wheel — a lower ratio — so its **effective rate at the wheel is lower** than its
nominal rate; wheel rate scales with the **square** of the ratio. Motion ratio scales **both**
effective spring rate and effective damping — the same spring or oil gives a different wheel rate as
the mounting changes, and it can vary through the travel.

**Moved by:** spring/damper mounting position on the arm.
**Affects:** effective [[spring-rate]] and [[damping]] at the wheel.
