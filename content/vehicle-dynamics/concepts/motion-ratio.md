## Motion ratio
**Physics.** The ratio between how far the **wheel** moves and how far the **spring/damper** moves —
set by where the unit mounts on the arm. A unit mounted further inboard moves less than the wheel, so
its **effective rate at the wheel is lower** than its nominal rate. Motion ratio scales **both**
effective spring rate and effective damping — the same spring or oil gives a different wheel rate as
the mounting changes, and it can vary through the travel.

**Moved by:** spring/damper mounting position on the arm.
**Affects:** effective [[spring-rate]] and [[damping]] at the wheel.
