## Friction circle (one grip budget, shared)
**Physics.** A tyre has **one** grip budget at any instant, and cornering and driving/braking both
spend it. Grip used lengthways — putting power down, slowing the car — is grip that is no longer
available sideways, and the reverse. A tyre already at its limit sideways has nothing left for
throttle; a tyre spending everything on drive has nothing left to hold a line.

So the same tyre that grips fine at steady throttle can let go when drive force is added at the same
lean, with **no change in load and no change in setup**. That is why "it only steps out when I pick
up the throttle" is a different problem from "it slides mid-corner", and why the fix for one is
often not the fix for the other.

The budget itself scales with vertical load — see [[tyre-load-sensitivity]] for how that part
behaves.

A property of the tyre itself, not of any adjustment.
