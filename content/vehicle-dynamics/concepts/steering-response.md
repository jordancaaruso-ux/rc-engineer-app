## Response — the angle the car gives the tyre
**Concepts:** [[grip-curve]], [[toe-and-scrub]], [[loaded-wheel]]

*(Founder framework, 2026-08-29. Described here once; the lever pages only say how they move it.)*

The driver's input reaches the tyre as a **wheel angle**, and what the driver feels as "steering" is
two things in series: **the angle the car gives the tyre, and the grip the tyre gives back.** The
second is the grip curve ([[grip-curve]]). The first is the steering geometry, and it acts before any
load has moved: static toe ([[toe]]) sets where the front wheels start; bump steer
([[bump-steer-toe-gain]]) how that angle changes as the front compresses; Ackermann
([[steering-geometry-ackermann]]) how the two fronts differ as the wheel is turned; caster
([[caster]]) the camber the wheel takes on as it steers, and how hard it wants to come back to
centre. A change to any of these moves how much the car does for a given input; a change to the grip
levers moves what the tyre does with it. Both are felt as steering.

**The first input is the geometry's before it is the grip's** (founder, 2026-09-09). The angle the
car gives the tyre is there the instant the wheel turns, before any load has moved; the grip levers
act through load transfer and its timing ([[bite-hold]]), which has not started yet. So a driver
asking about initial steering — more of it, or a calmer, smoother first input — is asking about the
angle first: toe, bump steer, Ackermann and caster are the levers, and the grip levers come after
them.

**The four are not equals** (founder, 2026-09-14). Toe and bump steer are small fixed angles, and a
small fixed angle counts most when the steering input is small — the first input — and is swamped
once the driver is holding lock in the middle. Caster and Ackermann do the opposite: what they do
grows with how far the wheel is turned, so they reach into the middle and the exit. A complaint about
the first input alone goes to toe or bump steer first; caster and Ackermann are the levers when the
driver has named the rest of the corner too.

**Moved by:** static toe [[toe]], bump steer [[bump-steer-toe-gain]], Ackermann
[[steering-geometry-ackermann]], caster [[caster]].
