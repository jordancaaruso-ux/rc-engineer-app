## Polar moment (yaw inertia)
**Physics.** Polar moment is the car's resistance to **yaw** — rotating about its vertical axis. It's
set by **how far the mass sits from the centre**: mass gathered **near the CoG** gives a **low** polar
moment, mass out **toward the ends** a **high** one. For a given yaw effort, how fast the car starts —
and stops — rotating goes **inversely** with it: low → **quick, eager** direction changes; high →
**steadier, lazier** yaw that resists being turned and resists being straightened.

**Moved by:** ballast placement, battery / motor position, weight distribution.
