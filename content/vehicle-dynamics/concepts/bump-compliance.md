## Bump compliance
**Physics.** A tyre grips most consistently when the **vertical load** pressing it into the road stays
**steady**. A rough or bumpy surface throws that load up and down. The suspension can **absorb** those
inputs — the wheel moves, less reaches the chassis, the load stays steadier — or **pass them through**,
so the load spikes and drops and the tyre can skip or briefly go **light** (no load, no grip). How much
it absorbs is set by how freely the wheel can move for a road input: how far the **spring** gives, how
the **damper** meters that movement, and whether **droop** lets the wheel extend into a dip. A tyre held
at steadier load keeps more of its grip — see [[tyre-load-sensitivity]].
