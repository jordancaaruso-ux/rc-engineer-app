## Roll stiffness (elastic)
**Physics.** How strongly the car resists **body roll** through its **springs and anti-roll bars** —
the elastic path, as opposed to the geometry (that's [[roll-center]]). Springs resist both **heave**
(both wheels moving up together) and roll; an **ARB resists roll only** — it does nothing when both
wheels move together. It sets two things:
- **How much the car rolls** for a given cornering load (the elastic, slow path of [[load-transfer]]).
- **Front/rear split** — the balance of roll stiffness front vs rear sets how much of the total load
  transfer happens at the front versus the rear.

**Moved by:** springs [[spring-rate]] (heave + roll), ARBs [[arb]] (roll only).
**Affects:** [[load-transfer]] (the elastic path + the front/rear split).
