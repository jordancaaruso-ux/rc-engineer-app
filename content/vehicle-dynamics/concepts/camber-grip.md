## Camber and grip
**Physics.** A tyre makes its best **lateral grip** at a certain **effective camber** (its static camber
plus [[camber-gain]] in roll) — enough tilt to keep the loaded contact patch working, not so much that
the patch shrinks. More camber builds peak lateral grip **up to that optimum**, then grip **falls off**,
often sharply; less camber gives a **lower peak** but a **more progressive** slip. A cambered tyre also
makes a little lateral force on its own (**camber thrust**) even before it slips, but only about a
**tenth per degree** of what slip angle gives — a minor add on top. Where the optimum sits varies a lot
**by tyre** — found by testing, not a fixed number.

A property of the tyre itself.
