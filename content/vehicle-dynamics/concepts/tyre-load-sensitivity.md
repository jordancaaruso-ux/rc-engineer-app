## Tyre load sensitivity
**Physics.** A tyre makes more grip the more vertical load it carries — but **less than
proportionally**: double the load and you get less than double the grip; grip-per-unit-load falls as
load rises. So loading one tyre of a pair much harder than the other makes **less grip from the pair**
than sharing the load evenly would, all else equal.

A property of the tyre itself.
