## Oil viscosity vs temperature

**Physics.** Oil thins as it heats and thickens as it cools — lower viscosity hot, higher
viscosity cold. So the same nominal oil gives **more damping when cold** (early in a run, cold
track) and **less as it warms**. A property of the fluid, independent of car or setup.

**Affects:** damper oil [[damper-oil]] (`damper_oil_front`, `damper_oil_rear`), diff oil
[[diff-and-driveline]], any silicone fluid.
