## Bodyshell (aero)
**Keys:** `rear_hrb_setting`, `body_position_from_windshield`
**Concepts:** [[aero-load]]

**Physics.** The shell is the car's **main aero device**. Raising its rear, or moving it fore/aft, changes
**how much aero load the body makes and where it acts** — all scaling with **speed²** ([[aero-load]]).
Body **choice** (shell model) is a large aero decision of its own.
