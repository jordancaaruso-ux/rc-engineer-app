## Chassis flex (general + front/rear)
**Concepts:** [[chassis-flex]]

**Physics.** How much the chassis **twists and bends** under load — see [[chassis-flex]]. Front vs rear
flex biases which end is softened.

## Chassis material
**Key:** `chassis`
**Concepts:** [[chassis-flex]], [[load-transfer]]

**Physics.** The plate material/construction sets baseline **flex** ([[chassis-flex]]) and can also change
**CoG height** ([[load-transfer]]) — e.g. a denser steel plate sits mass lower than carbon. Match part
numbers to your manufacturer sheet.

## Top decks, brace screws, deck cuts
**Keys:** `top_deck_front`, `top_deck_rear`, `top_deck_screws`, `motor_mount_screws`, `top_deck_cuts`
**Concepts:** [[chassis-flex]]

**Physics.** Local **flex** adjusters ([[chassis-flex]]): a wider/stiffer top deck, **more engaged
screws**, and **fewer cuts** each add stiffness in their zone; the reverse adds flex. Front-half adjusters
bias front flex, rear-half rear. The A–H grid and part names are kit-specific — use your sheet.
