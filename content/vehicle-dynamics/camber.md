## Camber
**Keys:** `camber_front`, `camber_rear`
**Concepts:** [[camber-grip]], [[camber-gain]]

**Physics.** Camber is the **tilt** of the tyre relative to the road. More **negative** camber tilts the
top of the tyre inward, helping the **outside** tyre stay nearer flat as the car rolls. The tyre runs
its **static** camber plus [[camber-gain]] in roll; what that effective tilt does to grip is [[camber-grip]].
