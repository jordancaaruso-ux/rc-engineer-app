## Spring rate
**Keys:** `spring_front`, `spring_rear`
**Concepts:** [[roll-stiffness]], [[bite-hold]], [[motion-ratio]], [[tyre-load-sensitivity]], [[bump-compliance]]

**Physics.** A higher spring rate resists both **heave** (both wheels together) and **roll** more at
that end, and lets that end take load **faster** (it rolls less, so load arrives sooner).
