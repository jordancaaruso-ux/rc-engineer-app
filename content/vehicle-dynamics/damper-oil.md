## Damper oil (thicker vs lighter)
**Keys:** `damper_oil_front`, `damper_oil_rear`
**Concepts:** [[damping]], [[bite-hold]], [[oil-viscosity-temperature]], [[bump-compliance]]

**Physics.** Thicker oil raises the **damping force** for a given shaft speed (pistons and holes shape
the curve); lighter lowers it. It's the main knob that sets how much [[damping]] you have.
