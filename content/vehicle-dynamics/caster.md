## Front caster
**Keys:** `caster_front`
**Concepts:** [[camber-grip]], [[steering-response]], [[bite-hold]]

**Physics.** Front caster tilts the **steering axis** back, so the contact patch trails behind where that
axis meets the ground (**mechanical trail**). Two things follow as you steer: the front wheel **gains
camber** (leaning into the corner — feeds [[camber-grip]]), and cornering force acting on the trail
creates a **self-aligning torque** that centres the steering. Most of caster's effect shows up **with
steering**, not at centre.

## Rear caster
**Keys:** `caster_rear`
**Concepts:** [[toe-and-scrub]]

**Physics.** Rear caster acts partly like a small **wheelbase** change (shifts the effective fore-aft
distance between the contact patches) and **interacts with rear toe through travel** — change it and the
rear toe-gain curve ([[toe-and-scrub]]) usually needs re-trimming to match.
